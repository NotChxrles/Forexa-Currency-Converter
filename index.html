Clear answers.

Whether you’re planning a trip, shopping abroad or sending money home, start with the essentials.

How does Forexa calculate a conversion?

Choose the currency you have, the currency you need and an amount. Forexa multiplies that amount by the exchange rate. For example, if a rate were 0.90 EUR per USD, 100 USD would convert to 90 EUR before fees. This is an illustration, not a current quote.

How fresh are these exchange rates?

Rates are supplied by ExchangeRate-API and updated once a day. The date below the converter is the provider’s rate timestamp, shown in UTC. Forexa reuses that daily data to load quickly. If fresh data cannot be reached, a clearly labelled saved rate may be shown for up to seven days.

Why is my bank’s rate different?

Forexa shows an indicative reference rate. A bank or transfer service may use a different rate, include a markup and charge additional fees. Open “Account for a transfer fee” to model fees you already know, then check the provider’s final quote before making a transfer.

Can I exchange or send money here?

No. Forexa is a free currency calculator, not a bank or transfer service. It helps you estimate and compare amounts. It does not hold funds or process payments.

How do I save my favourite pairs?

Choose two currencies and select “Save pair”. Up to six pairs can be kept in Your shortcuts. Select “Saved” on the current pair to remove it. Your shortcuts and theme stay in this browser when local storage is available; they do not sync between devices.

Your privacy, simply.

Your amounts and fee estimates are calculated in your browser and are not sent to the exchange-rate provider. Forexa stores your theme, saved currency pairs and a copy of daily rates on this device.

To obtain rates, your browser connects to ExchangeRate-API, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Read the provider’s terms. This version of Forexa uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Clearing saved data resets shortcuts and theme and removes the stored rates. Rates currently in memory remain available until you leave this page.
